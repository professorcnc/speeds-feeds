Q10: Concept

Reference

Formulas

RPM = (SFM × 3.82) / Diameter

Feed (milling) = RPM × flutes × chip load

Chip load = Feed ÷ (RPM × flutes)

Feed (turning) = RPM × IPR

Rules

Speed → controls heat
Feed → controls load

Low chip load → rubbing (bad)
High chip load → breakage (bad)

Quick Values

Aluminum chip load: 0.003–0.006
Steel chip load: 0.001–0.003
