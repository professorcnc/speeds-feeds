Q10 – Concept

What controls HEAT?

Reference Panel

Formulas

RPM = (SFM × 3.82) / Diameter

Feed (milling) = RPM × flutes × chip load

Chip load = Feed / (RPM × flutes)

Feed (turning) = RPM × IPR

Key Concepts

Speed = Heat
Feed = Load

Low chip load = rubbing → heat
High chip load = breakage

Typical Chip Loads

Aluminum: 0.003 – 0.006
Steel: 0.0015 – 0.003
